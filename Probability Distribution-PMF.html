Probability Distribution क्या होता है?

किसी random experiment के सभी possible outcomes की probability को व्यवस्थित तरीके से लिखना ही probability distribution कहलाता है।

सबसे आसान उदाहरण

एक coin toss किया।

Possible outcomes:

  • Head
  • Tail

दोनों की probability:

\[P(H) = \frac{1}{2}\]
\[P(H) = \frac{1}{2}\]

यही एक simple probability distribution है।

Random Variable क्या होता है?

Outcome को number देना।

  • Head = 0
  • Tail = 1

तो distribution:

Question

Three dice are thrown simultaneously.
Let X be the number of times 3 appears.
Find the probability distribution of X.

Solution

Possible Outcome:

X = Numbers of 3

\[P(X = x) = {}^nC_x\ p^x\ q^{n-x}\]
\[P(X = 0) = {}^3C_0 \times \left( \frac{1}{6} \right)^0 \times \left( \frac{5}{6} \right)^{3-0}\]
\[= 1 \times 1 \times \frac{5}{6} \times \frac{5}{6} \times \frac{5}{6}\]
\[P(X = 0) = \frac{125}{216}\]
\[P(X = 1) = {}^3C_1 \times \left( \frac{1}{6} \right)^1 \times \left( \frac{5}{6} \right)^{3-1}\]
\[= 3 \times \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{5}{6} \times \frac{5}{6}\]
\[P(X = 1) = \frac{75}{216}\]
\[P(X = 2) = {}^3C_2 \times \left( \frac{1}{6} \right)^2 \times \left( \frac{5}{6} \right)^{3-2}\]
\[= 3 \times \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{5}{6}\]
\[P(X = 2) = \frac{15}{216}\]
\[P(X = 3) = {}^3C_3 \times \left( \frac{1}{6} \right)^3 \times \left( \frac{5}{6} \right)^{3-3}\]
\[= 1 \times \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{1}{6} \times 1\]
\[P(X = 3) = \frac{1}{216}\]
X P(X)
0 125\216
1 75\216
2 15\216
3 1\216

Let's varify

\[\frac{125}{216} \times \frac{75}{216} \times \frac{15}{216} \times \frac{1}{216} = \frac{216}{216} = 1\]

Question

Two cards are drawn simultaneously from a standard deck of 52 cards.
Let X be the number of Kings obtained.
Find the probability distribution of X.

Solution

X = Numbers of 2 Kings

King = 0

48 non-King cards में से 2 चुनो:

\[P(X = 0) = \frac{{}^{48}C_2}{{}^{52}C_2}\]
\[P(X = 0)= \frac{{\frac{48 \times 47 \times 46!}{46! \times 2 \times 1}}}{\frac{52 \times 51 \times 50!}{50! \times 2 \times 1}} = \frac{1128}{1326}\]

ठीक 1 King।

1 King और 1 non-King:

\[P(X = 1) = \frac{{}^4C_1\ {}^{52 - 4}C_1}{{}^{52}C_2}\]
\[P(X = 1) = \frac{4 \times 48}{1326} = \frac{192}{1326}\]

दोनों King।

\[P(X = 2) = \frac{{}^4C_2}{{}^{52}C_2} = \frac{6}{1326}\]
X P(X)
0 1128\1326
1 192\1326
2 6\1326

Verification

\[\frac{1128 + 192 + 6}{1326} = \frac{1326}{1326} = 1\]
